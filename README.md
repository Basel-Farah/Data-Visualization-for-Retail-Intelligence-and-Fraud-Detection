# Data-Visualization-for-Retail-Intelligence-and-Fraud-Detection
Retail Intelligence & Fraud Detection Dashboard
An end-to-end data visualization and business intelligence project built in Tableau to monitor retail performance, analyze customer behavior, and detect anomalous transactions across global markets.
📊 Overview
This project consists of three interactive dashboards designed to provide executives, marketing teams, and risk analysts with actionable insights into sales growth, customer demographics, and transaction security.
1. Executive Overview
Provides a high-level snapshot of overall business health and financial KPIs:
•	Key Metrics: Tracks Total Orders (45,103), Total Revenue ($1.26B), Average Order Value ($28K), and overall Profit Margin (35%).
•	Monthly Sales Performance: Visualizes revenue trends over the fiscal year, highlighting Q3 growth peaks and Q4 seasonal fluctuations.
•	Product & Regional Breakdown: Identifies top-performing product categories (led by Clothing at $262M and Sports at $260M) and compares regional revenue and profitability across North America, Europe, Asia, South America, and Africa.
2. Customer & Product Intelligence
Focuses on customer segmentation, acquisition channels, and behavioral patterns:
•	Acquisition & Demographics: Evaluates customer distribution across marketing channels (Social Media, Paid Ads, Organic Search, Referral, Email) and segments users by age group and gender.
•	Category Preferences: Maps age brackets against product categories to reveal target demographic affinities.
•	Retention & Basket Analysis: Tracks average customer tenure (24.06 months), average basket size (10.50 items), and discount adoption rates across tiers.
3. Fraud & Risk Analysis
Monitors transaction anomalies and identifies high-risk patterns to mitigate financial loss:
•	Risk KPIs: Highlights a 16.05% overall fraud rate (7,239 flagged orders out of 45,103), accounting for $202.07M in flagged revenue and an average fraud risk score of 49.94.
•	Anomaly Detection: Breaks down flagged transactions by payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, Debit Card, Crypto, Bank Transfer), device type (Desktop, Mobile, Tablet), and geographical origin.
•	Temporal & Behavioral Patterns: Correlates order volume against fraud rates across different times of the day (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night) and compares average risk scores across customer segments (Regular, VIP, New).
